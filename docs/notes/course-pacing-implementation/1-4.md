## Boundaries and retained evidence

Module 1 now has six pages: hexadecimal, numerical readings and their practice, arithmetic on
those bits, loading a constant, constructing a constant, and register widths. The extra arithmetic
page keeps the fuel subtraction, adding-12 prediction and signed/unsigned compiler comparison
out of the readings practice. Hexadecimal uses the existing computed register reader with a
`hexOnly` option and the existing hexadecimal-entry control. Its initial pattern still comes
from the original model run. It shows no signed/unsigned labels before those readings are taught.

Addition retains its complete calculation, flag practice and stale-flags experiment, with a
shorter later explanation. Subtraction finishes the elapsed-time writing and boundary checks
before its explicitly titled adder drill-down and later signed-negation activity. Both original
adder views remain; their column controls and the counter ring are retained. The counter's
normal-advancement and less-than-one-full-turn assumptions remain visible.

Masks and fields have separate pages and separate closing checks. The original two mask
cases remain together on `masks`; the field case moves to `trace-gear-field` on
`reading-and-writing-fields`. The field plan, construction, own-code stepping, GCC comparison
and missing-clear prediction remain together. The field reader has no remote mask prediction
gate. The shifted-register addition demonstration now sits immediately before the compiler's
division correction rather than interrupting the basic shift/division comparison.

Comparison keeps the complete compare → flags → condition → select mechanism, the writing
exercise, wrong-condition experiment and four-condition check. `cc` and `lt` are now explicitly
defined before construction. The condition-family explorer, Boolean/compiler cases and
strict/inclusive reflection form the following page, before branches need them. Branches
retains its existing trace, paths, construction and boundary experiment. No new assembly or
compiler record was invented.

The managing agent accepted further splits identified by `/root/completion_learner` on the first
integrated build. The current source therefore contains **19 units** across Modules 1–4:

  `flags-and-overflow` owns the unchanged-flag trace and smallest-overflow application.
- The counter ring, elapsed-time writing and a counter-only closing question stay on
