## Boundaries and retained activities

- Module 5 has five units: follow the original upward trace and count fresh runs; write the
  upward loop and examine its changed branch; use subtraction flags in a countdown; compare
  testing before/after the body and the always-true unsigned condition; stop when useful work
  ends. The two single-input failure bars are omitted. Their complete steppers, predictions,
  wraparound explanation, C qualification and trace-limit distinction remain together.
- Module 6 separates the first address/load investigation and save task from transfer widths.
  `transfer-widths` keeps the wrong-store prediction, narrow-store comparison, eight-byte
  operations and copy task together. The load comparison, gradient task, wrong-load experiment,
  offsets and mixed record stay on the load route. Register-only extension, casts, the signed
  conversion qualification and extended addition have a later focused unit. Moved steppers
  have no dependency on a prediction from another page.
- Module 7 separates selecting one element from moving through an array. The addressing-form
  comparison moves to traversal, followed by pointer arithmetic, the complete sum trace, the
  end-address explanation and sum task. Selection retains its missing-scale prediction and
  trace. The strings investigation is intact.
- Module 8 gives low products/multiply-add, widening, product halves, core division/remainders,
  consumption and constant division separate completion points. The widening task precedes
  product halves. The full `msub` explanation and worked step move directly before remainder
  derivation. The consumption contract retains units, zero distance, width planning and the
  incoming `w1`/`x1` guarantee. Constant division follows that application.
- Module 9 completes the small returning call, frame mechanics, first task and lost-link
  experiment before the reserve-preservation prediction, larger frame and task. The latter
  retains argument-register and full-width preservation rules. `recursive-calls` keeps the
  complete sum trace, deepest-frame view and base-branch repair. `making-recursive-progress`
  then owns the unchanged-argument prediction and complete failure trace, progress explanation
  and `lit_count` task. Only the duplicated paragraph about return order is omitted.

Compiler and target notes accompany moved recorded listings. New term homes distinguish
array selection from pre/post-index addressing, load extension from casts, and widening products
from high halves. Instruction homes are reported to the managing agent for `TAUGHT` integration.
All mounted challenge contracts, reference programs and tests retain their source definitions.
