## User request and outcome

Apply the approved AArch64 type introduction as a target-qualified lead, two parallel bullets for signed and unsigned readings, then the C spelling note. Review all published pages for other opportunities to make existing content easier to scan and follow with layout.

The observable aim is to identify inputs, rules and actions without retaining a dense prose list in working memory. Parallel facts may become bullets; genuine procedures may become ordered lists; small comparisons may become compact tables when they remain usable on phones. Causal explanations stay in connected paragraphs. No indiscriminate bullet conversion, new miniature headings, hidden required guidance, new arithmetic programs or changes to challenges/prediction gates are authorised by this layout task.
