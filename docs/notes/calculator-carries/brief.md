Checked source facts: packages/machine/src/run.ts addWithCarry records carries as (a XOR b XOR raw sum) masked to width. Carry bit i is the carry into column i; ordinary addition starts with carry into bit 0 equal to 0. Carry out is the raw sum's bit at the selected width. C equals that carry out. V is 1 exactly when carry into the sign bit differs from carry out. These flags depend on operand patterns and width, not the selected signed/unsigned decimal reading.
