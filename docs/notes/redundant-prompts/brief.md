Copyright © 2026 Christopher Snow

## Request and checked facts

The author asks to remove the standalone questions after activities in Subtraction and reuse,
and equivalents elsewhere: “In the subtraction, which old value was read before being
replaced?” and “Would swapping the subtraction inputs give the same result?” These appear
as construction/failureExperiment prose-only sections in fundamentals.prose.ts. The existing
worked example, operand explanation and graded fresh-input exercise already teach and check
read-before-write and subtraction input order; challenge hints give specific feedback.

One addition and Instructions in sequence have similar prose-only follow-up sections. The
course uses selected section layouts, so redundant blocks and headings can be omitted without
changing the platform. Most later lessons have real construction exercises, failure experiments,
prediction gates and closing questions with answer disclosures. Judge those against their actual
activity: do not delete sections merely because their titles or prose contain questions.

Audit every current lesson, including closing questions and prose-only template sections.
Remove prompts that only repeat the preceding activity or ask the learner to mentally perform
a comparison with no useful outcome/feedback. Remove the associated heading/section when empty.
Keep meaningful first-use teaching, necessary qualifiers, supported prediction/experiment prompts,
new transfer practice, reference lookups and real physical-board evidence tasks. Avoid replacing
removed filler with new summaries or exercises. Preserve programs, figures, challenges, gates,
saved-work identities and the platform unchanged.

## Process and ownership

Follow CLAUDE.md, docs/authoring.md and both passes in docs/style.md. Read complete lessons and
existing activities before deciding. A separate drafting agent owns editorial content, section
selection and canonical prose synchronisation. Record a concise all-lesson audit with decisions
and evidence, including retained question types. The root owns tests, authoring-rule updates and
integration. Independent learner/technical reviewers assess final source and built pages without
using the draft author's rationale as their verdict. Check changed pages at phone/desktop in
actual light/dark themes and preserve useful interactions. Keep full and focused check histories
separate. Do not touch the unrelated untracked Pi sources brief.
