A compiler can choose this form of `add` for an addition by a fixed amount such as 4096. You can also choose it when writing assembly. The constant goes in the instruction, so you do not need another register to hold it.

Compiler or assembly programmer → chooses a suitable form → `add` instruction → executed by → Processor

Accessible description: A compiler or a person writing assembly chooses a suitable form of the add instruction before the processor executes it.
